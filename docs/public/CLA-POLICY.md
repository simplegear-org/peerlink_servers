# Contributor License Policy

POLICY / DRAFT

NOT A SIGNABLE AGREEMENT

PeerLink Servers intends to support both open-source and separate commercial
licensing models.

External contributions may therefore require a Contributor License Agreement
providing the project with sufficient rights to distribute accepted
contributions under the project's open-source license and, where applicable,
separate commercial terms.

Contributors retain whatever rights the final agreement specifies.

The final CLA text requires legal review before activation.

Until the CLA process is formally activated, maintainers should not accept
non-trivial external code contributions where ownership or relicensing rights
would become ambiguous.
